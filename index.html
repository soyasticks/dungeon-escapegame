    How to Play

    Goal

    Collect every key on the level to unlock the gate, then step onto it to escape. Three levels, each larger with more enemies and traps.

    Controls

    W A S D / ArrowsMove
    P / EscPause / resume
    RRestart level
    VShow / hide enemy AI paths
    MSound on / off
    EnterContinue on level screens

    The dungeon

    • Stone floor — cost 1
    • Mud — cost 3, slows you
    • Deep water — cost 5, slows you more
    • Spike trap — hurts when spikes are up
    • Gems +50, chest +150, key +100
    • Potion — restores one heart

    The enemies

    Each monster plans its route with one search algorithm (its label shows which). They patrol until you come close, then hunt you. Contact costs a heart. Press V to watch their planned paths.
